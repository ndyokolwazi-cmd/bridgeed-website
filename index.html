The Problem We Are Solving

Teachers want to succeed.
They just need the right support.

The technology to transform school administration exists and most schools can afford it. The structured, sustained training to make it work in under-resourced communities does not — until now.

CAPS Compliance

Most rural teachers are producing DBE reports manually, with errors that put school compliance at risk. The tools to fix this already exist. What is missing is the training.

Digital Skills Gap

The majority of rural and township teachers have never received hands-on training on a school management platform. Not once, in their entire career.

Parent Communication

Schools in townships and rural communities have no reliable channel to communicate with parents about learner progress, attendance or school announcements.

Administrative Burden

Hours spent weekly on paperwork that a proper system would reduce to minutes. That time belongs in the classroom, not on a manual register.

Our Approach

A four-phase programme.
Built for lasting change.

We do not run one-day workshops and leave. We go on-site, we train in the teachers' home languages, and we stay for the full implementation cycle until the school can run independently.

1
Foundation
5 days on-site

All teachers. At the school. Hands-on training in their preferred language. No remote sessions, no Zoom calls.

2
Consolidation
1 to 2 days, 3 weeks later

We return to address the real questions that only arise once teachers start using the system in practice.

3
Champion Support
Monthly for 3 months

Ongoing support for the school's identified champion teacher via WhatsApp and regular check-in sessions.

4
Certification
End of Month 4

Practical assessment. Recognised certificates for every qualifying teacher. Official myEncore Certified School status.

5-Day Training Breakdown

Every day. Every module.
Every teacher.

Training builds progressively, from basic navigation to full CAPS compliance and parent communication. No prior technology experience required.

Day 1
Platform Setup & Navigation
  • Device setup and login
  • School profile configuration
  • Dashboard navigation
  • Teacher profiles and roles
Day 2
Attendance & Class Management
  • Digital attendance taking
  • Timetable setup
  • Class and subject linking
  • Yard duty and activity tools
Day 3
Marks & CAPS Tracking
  • Mark capture and weighting
  • CAPS curriculum alignment
  • Learner progress views
  • Intervention flags
Day 4
Reports & Parent Communication
  • Automated report generation
  • Parent portal activation
  • Homework and announcements
  • Communication history
Day 5
Compliance & Admin
  • SASAMS sync and validation
  • DBE compliance checks
  • Discipline and merit tools
  • Champion identification

Township Schools

How It's Funded

Schools pay nothing.
Change-makers fund the change.

We actively partner with corporates, foundations and government programmes to ensure every school receives world-class training at zero cost.

🏢

Corporate CSI

South African companies with B-BBEE obligations fund teacher development as part of their Social and Enterprise Development commitments. Education is the most fundable CSI category.

📋

ETDP SETA

BridgeEd is pursuing ETDP SETA accreditation, enabling schools and districts to access SETA funding for teacher training that counts toward CPD requirements.

🌍

Development Funders

We partner with education-focused foundations and international development organisations committed to closing the technology gap in South African schools.

📱

myEncore Partnership

As an authorised myEncore training partner, we work with the platform team to co-fund access to schools in communities they cannot currently reach directly.

🤝

NGO and Foundation Grants

We actively apply for education grants from SA-focused foundations including Zenex, Ikusasa and the Michael and Susan Dell Foundation.

🎯

BackaBuddy Campaigns

School-specific crowdfunding campaigns invite community members, alumni and parents to directly sponsor digital training for their local school.
